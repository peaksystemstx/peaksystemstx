Our specialty

Solar Panel Care

Panels that sit dirty produce less power and wear out faster. Our core service is keeping your solar investment clean, protected, and performing at its best.

Solar Panel Care

Dust, pollen, water spots, and bird droppings block sunlight from reaching your panels, and rain alone won't wash the film away — so a dirty system steadily produces less power than it should. Our safe, panel-friendly cleaning removes that buildup to restore energy production, and regular cleanings help prevent the hot spots and long-term wear that shorten a system's life. Every visit is also a chance to catch cracked glass, loose hardware, or nesting birds before they turn into expensive repairs.

Solar Bird Proofing

Birds nesting beneath panels leave droppings across the array, pack flammable debris against your roof, and can damage wiring. Professional mesh exclusion keeps them out permanently — protecting your production, your equipment, and your roof.
